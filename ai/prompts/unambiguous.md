Identify ambiguities, internal inconsistencies, and missing constraints or decisions that could change behavior, scope, acceptance criteria, or consequential choices.

- Investigate relevant code and docs before asking. Distinguish established facts from assumptions and decisions about intent or tradeoffs; existing behavior is evidence, not necessarily the desired requirement.

- Respect settled decisions; revisit them only when new evidence reveals a material conflict, and explain why.
- Stop when no material blockers remain. Summarize resolved decisions and explicitly list any accepted assumptions or deferred decisions; do not silently treat blockers as resolved.
- This is a clarification task, not authorization to edit files or implement changes.
